LA Museum & Aquarium Explorer

About this map

This map shows museums and aquariums across Los Angeles County. Locations and venue details load directly from LA County’s public GIS layer.

Adapted from Esri’s Intro to FeatureLayer sample, it uses a Los Angeles starting view, a gray basemap, and purple markers. Click a marker for the venue’s name, address, city, and category, or use Home to return to the starting view.
